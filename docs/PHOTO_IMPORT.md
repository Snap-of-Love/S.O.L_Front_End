# Importing photos

Choose **Import photos**, or drop JPEG, PNG or WebP files onto the import area. Select one to eight files, up to 12 MB and 36 megapixels each. HEIC and SVG are not supported; export them to JPEG/PNG first.

The studio prepares one frame per imported photo and picks a layout with exactly that number of slots. No extra photo is silently discarded. Imports are decoded sequentially and resized to at most 1600 pixels on the longer side to keep editing manageable. Transparency in photographs is composited on white; transparent custom frame artwork uses a separate template importer.

An invalid batch leaves the previous session intact. You can replace a particular slot later. Imported photos have no burst frames; GIF motion is available for camera burst captures.

Importing and editing happens on this device. Local downloads do not require an account or backend. Creating a cloud share is an explicit separate action.
