# Per-photo composition

Choose a thumbnail, then use Zoom, Horizontal alignment and Vertical alignment. Rotate changes the image in quarter turns; Mirror is independent of the camera's mirrored preview. Reset affects only the chosen photo. Sliders work with arrow keys.

Alignment moves within the available overflow: at 1× zoom a dimension already filling its slot cannot move further. Cover scaling prevents empty borders, including after rotation. These settings are saved with local drafts and applied by one renderer to preview, PNG/JPEG, animated GIF and cloud copies. Replacing or retaking a photo resets its crop. Changing paper layouts preserves each photo's settings.
