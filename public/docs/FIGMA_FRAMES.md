# Your frame designs: Figma → S.O.L

1. Open **Make your own frame · Figma friendly**, below the studio. Name the frame and set its width and height in pixels. A 2 × 6 inch strip at 300 pixels per inch uses **600 × 1800 px**. A 4 × 6 print uses **1200 × 1800 px**. Print services may require bleed and their own specifications; confirm with the printer. The app exports pixels and does not embed print DPI metadata.
2. Enter each photo opening's Left (X), Top (Y), Width and Height. Coordinates begin at the paper's top-left. Add 1–8 openings; keep them inside the paper without overlap. The default 600 × 1800 strip has four 520 × 350 openings, at X=40 and Y=50, 440, 830, 1220.
3. Download **Figma guide** and **Export template JSON**. Drag the SVG into Figma. Create a top-level Frame using the same W and H; place the guide at X=0, Y=0 inside it. Keep the guide at 100% scale. Its photo layers include the exact coordinates in their names.
4. Design your paper, text and illustrations above the guide. Make actual transparent photo openings: for a solid paper rectangle, place matching photo rectangles above it and use Figma's Boolean **Subtract selection**. Remove the Frame's background fill. Hide or delete the pink photo guide and guide paper before exporting. A white rectangle is opaque and will hide a photo.
5. Select the outer Frame, choose PNG export at **1×** (600 × 1800 for this example) or **2×** (1200 × 3600), then export. Use PNG transparency. JPEG cannot preserve the openings. Keep artwork under 5 MB; the app supports up to 4 million logical paper pixels and 16 million PNG pixels. SVG artwork uploads are intentionally unsupported; the SVG download is a coordinate guide for Figma.
6. Return to S.O.L and **Import PNG artwork**. The app checks the PNG dimensions and transparency at each opening's center. Check the full preview yourself: this spot check cannot prove every pixel of every opening is clear. **Save & use frame** stores it on this browser. Capture or import exactly the frame's photo count. Crops, filters, stickers and drawings work normally. Custom frames replace the built-in brand/date/footer so your design controls that area; include any desired text in your artwork.
7. Export the template JSON after adding artwork. It includes the image and all coordinates; keep it as a backup or import it on another device. Up to 10 templates / 24 MB are stored locally. Clearing site data or using private browsing can remove them. Local draft recovery also carries the active frame.

If you already have a Figma design, set the editor to its outer Frame's exact pixel size and copy each opening's X/Y/W/H relative to that Frame. Do not include Figma's surrounding canvas coordinates. Export with transparent holes, then import PNG. Changing dimensions or slot coordinates in S.O.L removes the old artwork so it cannot silently become misaligned.

Figma's [official export instructions](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040028114-Export-static-designs-from-Figma) explain the export panel and scale settings.
